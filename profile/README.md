# Trashures

> One's trash is another's treasure.

Take a photo of a waste item and find out which bin it goes in, **for your exact location**.

Waste rules in Germany differ from district to district: a banana peel goes into the Biotonne in
Freiburg, but into the Restmüll bin in Waldkirch, 15 km away. Trashures recognizes the item,
finds the local waste authority for your postal code and answers with the local rule and its
official source.

**Status:** early prototype, piloting in Freiburg im Breisgau and Landkreis Emmendingen.
